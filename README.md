### CLIM-000237: Real-world AMOC stability boundary
- **Subfield:** Ocean circulation and heat uptake
- **Open:** Locate Atlantic overturning stability boundaries under realistic warming, freshwater input, forcing rates, and Southern Ocean coupling. Determine which modeled feedbacks apply to the observed ocean.
- **Status:** Multiple equilibria studied since Stommel’s 1961 model; 2024 collapse and 2025 resilience experiments produced contrasting conditional results.
- **Type:** theory
- **Why:** Constrains risks to heat transport, rainfall, and regional sea level.
- **Key works:** van Westen, Kliphuis and Dijkstra (2024), [“Physics-based early warning signal shows that AMOC is on tipping course”](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.adk1189); Baker et al. (2025), [“Continued Atlantic overturning circulation even under climate extremes”](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-024-08544-0).
- **Related:** Rate-induced tipping; bifurcation analysis with uncertain parameters.
